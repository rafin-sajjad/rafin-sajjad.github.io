Active Projects

SOAR-GFM: Grid-Forming Control Architecture

Development of SOAR-GFM, a novel grid-forming control architecture enabling autonomous inverter operation without reliance on a stiff grid reference. The controller implements a synchronizing oscillator with active disturbance-rejection and robust predictive inner-loop regulation for inverter-based resources (IBRs). Validated using real-time Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) testing on Typhoon HIL 101 and OPAL-RT OP4610XG platforms. Related U.S. patent pending (Dec. 2025).

Grid-Forming Control Active Disturbance Rejection Predictive Control HIL Validation

Physics-Informed AI Control for Inverter-Based Resources

Design of physics-informed, safety-constrained neural networks as real-time controllers for grid-forming inverters. The framework integrates transformer-based AI architectures with power system physics constraints to guarantee stability and feasibility during autonomous grid operation. Accepted at IEEE IAS Annual Meeting 2026 and IEEE ECCE 2026.

Physics-Informed Neural Networks Safety-Constrained Control Transformer Architecture Data-Driven Control

Sub-Harmonic Synchronous Machine Design

Novel electric machine topology employing sub-harmonic stator winding configurations — two-layer, three-layer, and dual-stator variants — with dual-inverter drives, consequent-pole permanent magnets, and Vernier machine principles. Targeting high-power transportation electrification. Results include 1 issued U.S. patent, 2 patent applications, and multiple IEEE Transactions on Magnetics papers.

Sub-Harmonic Machines PM-Assisted Design Dual Inverter Drive Transportation Electrification

HIL-Based Microgrid Cyber-Physical Security Testbed

Hardware-in-the-loop implementation and evaluation of grid-forming inverter controls under network disturbances, communication failures, and cyber attacks (denial-of-service, SCADA intrusions). Demonstrates resilience of microgrid architectures supported by ONR (Contract No. N00014-21-1-2493) and ARL (Contract No. W911NF2320229).

Cyber-Physical Security HIL Testbed Microgrid Resilience Denial-of-Service
