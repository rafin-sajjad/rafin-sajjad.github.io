Research & Teaching Vision

My long-term goal is to advance resilient, stable, intelligent, and secure power-electronic energy systems through grid-forming control, AI-assisted physics-informed control, electric machine innovation, and experimental validation. In parallel, I aim to educate future power engineers by integrating simulation, hardware validation, and research-driven learning into curricula for power electronics and energy systems.
